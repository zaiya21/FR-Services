    How to read this

    Checked by FR Services
    We have seen the original and confirmed it with the issuing office. We check three documents: DTI registration, the mayor's permit and BIR 2303.
    Company-submitted
    The company's own upload - either one of the three still awaiting our check, or a supporting document we do not review at all, such as an insurance policy or a vehicle OR/CR. Treat it as a claim, not proof.
    Expired
    The validity date has passed. A company cannot hide this by publishing it.
