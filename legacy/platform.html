Document review

FR Services verifies three documents, and only three: DTI registration, the mayor's permit and BIR 2303. Those establish that a business exists and is registered to trade - the question a renter is really asking. Approving one puts Checked by FR Services on the company's public page, so it means we have seen the original and confirmed it with the issuing office.

What a verdict does

This is the only screen on the platform that can write a review verdict. A company can publish and unpublish its own documents; it can never mark one verified.

Insurance policies, OR/CRs, PCAB, TESDA and the rest are the owner's own call on what to publish - 0 such documents are on file and none of them appear here. They are shown to renters as the company's own claim, never as something we stand behind. Reviewing a fleet's worth of OR/CRs would be a promise we could not keep.
If a company edits a reviewed document's number, dates or file, it returns here automatically - a verdict describes what we saw, not the row it was attached to.
