What publishing does, and what it doesn't

Publishing puts a document on your public page under Documents, where anyone can check it before booking. Three things are outside your control on purpose, because they are what make the section worth reading:

Who says it's genuine
FR Services, never you
Expiry dates
Computed from the date you enter
Document numbers
Masked unless you opt in

FR Services checks three documents and no others: DTI registration, your mayor's permit and BIR 2303. Add or materially edit one of those and it goes back to Awaiting review until our team confirms it with the issuing office.
Everything else - insurance, OR/CRs, PCAB, TESDA - is yours to publish or not. Renters see those as your own supporting documents rather than something we verified, which is the honest description of them.
